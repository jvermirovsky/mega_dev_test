Counts come from the reports and the tenant directory at render time. A report belongs to a problem by its tenant (locale and booking form), never by its subject line, its intake tag or how the reporter describes it.

The two problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. A day-first tenant's dates come out with day and month swapped. This is the bug #4471 describes. Reports come in by app, email and form.

hint: the booking form teaches the wrong order. Booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so people at a day-first tenant type the month first, and the form reads it their way. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants belong here. Fixing the parser does not touch this: the fix is the hint text or the form.

A few reports describe a date that is one day off instead of a month flip. The desk filed them by tenant, under parser, and footnotes them. They are counted once, in that row.

Why the merge hid this

  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line. The same subjects ("Wrong day on the invite", "Appointment moved by itself") come in for both problems.
  • Intake tags a report from the reporter's browser, before anyone looks at the tenant. The "tagged wrong" column shows how often that guess names the other problem.
  • The ticket export comes in pages that overlap, so the same report can arrive twice. One id is one report.

What to do

  • Keep one ticket per problem, each with the count shown above.
  • Decide a new matching rule: group by cause (tenant locale and booking form), not by subject. Write it as a new decision record that replaces 0001. Do not edit 0001.
  • Count reports by id before you quote a number.
